A single museum label from the year 3000, placed beside an ordinary object from today: a "Do Not Disturb" sign, which the curators of the future have confidently misread as a ward against cleaning spirits.

> Do Not Disturb sign, c. 2025. Hung outside hotel doors to ward off the cleaning spirits; guests believed the spirits could read.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) reads `entry.txt` sitting next to it, checks that the label is non-empty, a single line, and at most 200 characters, then copies its exact bytes to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` and prints the SHA-256 of the output.
